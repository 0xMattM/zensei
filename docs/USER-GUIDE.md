## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Connect Your Wallet

1. **Visit the ZenSei Web App**
   - Open your browser and navigate to ZenSei
   - You'll see a beautiful, zen-inspired landing page

2. **Click "Connect Wallet"**
   - Choose your preferred Sei wallet (Keplr, Compass, etc.)
   - Approve the connection in your wallet
   - Your wallet is now securely linked!

3. **Welcome Setup**
   - Tell ZenSei about your DeFi experience level
   - Set your risk tolerance (Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive)
   - Choose your primary goals (Income, Growth, Learning)

### Step 2: Meet Your AI Agents

Once connected, you'll see your **Agent Dashboard** where you can:
- View all 8 specialized agents
- See what each agent can do for you
- Check agent status and activity

### Step 3: Start Your First Conversation

Simply type in the chat box! Here are some great first messages:

- *"Hi ZenSei, show me my portfolio"*
- *"What's my SEI balance?"*
- *"Help me understand DeFi on Sei"*

## 🔐 Security Best Practices

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Common Questions

**Q: ZenSei isn't responding to my message**
A: Check your internet connection and try refreshing the page. If issues persist, our agents might be updating - try again in a few moments.

**Q: I don't see my transaction in my wallet**
A: Sei transactions are very fast, but sometimes wallets take a moment to update. Check the transaction hash on the Sei explorer for confirmation.

**Q: The AI doesn't understand my request**
A: Try rephrasing your question more specifically. For example, instead of "help me," try "show me my yield farming opportunities."

**Q: How do I know if a DeFi protocol is safe?**
A: Ask ZenSei directly! Say "Is [protocol name] safe?" and our Research and Expert agents will provide a comprehensive risk analysis.
